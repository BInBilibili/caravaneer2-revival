Revival

An optional data-driven battle animation pack using original artwork, poses and event timing without reducing resolution.

  • Bone, slot and attachment tracks for all eight weapon animation types: idle, walk, attack, hit and death.
  • Lossless compact part atlases with original-image fallback, plus transport animal reaction tracks.

Disabling this DLC and reloading restores the base battle implementation, including its original animation and effects path. DLC runtime, skeleton data and compact atlases are not loaded.

Enabled by default. Reload through the DLC screen after changing your selection. This pack does not rewrite characters or inventory in saves; a different DLC list may still trigger the existing compatibility notice.

This is a staged animation implementation, not a new artwork pack or a claim that all authoring tools and performance work are complete.
